How to Play

Sabotage

Deception party game. 1-2 players are secretly Saboteurs. The Guards must complete missions and find the Saboteurs.

Auction Chaos

Bid your own HP to win points. If you win the auction, you get the points but lose the HP you bid. Highest points at the end wins.

Liar's Canvas

Everyone receives a secret drawing prompt except the Liar. Draw lines in turns. Catch the Liar before they guess the prompt!

Word Bomb

A prompt appears (e.g. 'ka'). Type a word containing those letters before the bomb explodes to pass it.
